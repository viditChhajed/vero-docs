# Persuasion Patterns

A browser extension that notices persuasion techniques on shopping pages and asks a question
about them.

- [Privacy policy](./privacy.html)

The extension makes no network requests. Everything it notices stays on your device and is
deleted after 30 days. It asks for no site access when you install it; you grant one site at
a time and can revoke any of them at any time.
